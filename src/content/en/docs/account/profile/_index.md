**User profile** lets you update your personal account login details and contact information.

In the app:

- Layout title: **My profile**
- Page title: **Login & Contact**
- Subtitle: *Update your account login details and contact information.*

## Prerequisites

- An authenticated Cloud2Gether user session

## How to access

1. Sign in to Cloud2Gether
2. Open your user menu / profile entry in the app header (as provided by the layout)
3. Or open: <a href="https://app.cloud2gether.com/user/profile" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">https://app.cloud2gether.com/user/profile</a>

## What you can do

The page loads your user profile with **contacts** enabled. Typical actions include:

- Review and update login-related profile fields
- Manage contact information associated with your user

Save changes using the controls provided by the profile component in the UI.

{{< alert color="info" title="User vs Company" >}}
This page is **your** personal profile. Organization name, address, and logo are managed under [Company → Profile](../../company/profile/) (Owner role).
{{< /alert >}}
