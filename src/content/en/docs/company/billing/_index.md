**Billing** (sidebar label) opens **Billing & Subscriptions**, where you review the active plan, payment details, related subscriptions, and billing history.

## Prerequisites

- Access to **Company → Billing**
- An organization with a subscription record (Free or paid)

## How to access

1. Sign in to Cloud2Gether
2. In the left sidebar, expand **Company** → **Billing**
3. Or open: <a href="https://auth.cloud2gether.com/auth/login/password" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">https://auth.cloud2gether.com/auth/login/password</a>

## Subscription summary

The top card shows:

- **Organization** name and logo
- **Plan** name
- **Status**
- **Next payment** date
- **Payment method**

Actions:

- **Change plan** — navigates to [Plans](../plans/)
- **Cancel subscription** — available when cancellation is allowed; opens a confirmation flow

## Manage subscription tabs

Below the summary card:

### Subscriptions

- Search: *Search for a Cloud Account*
- Table of subscription rows (including price per month, start/end dates, and status)

### Billing

Filter invoices by:

- **Month**
- **Year**
- **Status** (Paid, Pending, Failed, Refunded)

Open **See details** on a billing row to review invoice details.
