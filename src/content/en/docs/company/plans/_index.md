**Plans** shows available Cloud2Gether subscription plans for your organization, your current plan, and actions to change or request a custom offering.

Page title in the app: **Plans**

## Prerequisites

- Access to **Company → Plans**
- Permission to change subscription (as enforced by the app)

## How to access

1. Sign in to Cloud2Gether
2. In the left sidebar, expand **Company** → **Plans**
3. Or open: <a href="https://auth.cloud2gether.com/auth/login/password" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">https://auth.cloud2gether.com/auth/login/password</a>
4. From **Billing**, click **Change plan**
5. From the Dashboard, use **Upgrade Plan** / **See plan details** when shown

## What you see

- Organization profile header with the current plan name
- A card for each available plan (name, price, features, and action button)
- A plan may show a **Most popular** badge
- **Need a Custom Plan?** banner with **Request a custom plan**

Plan cards list included features (with checkmarks and short descriptions when provided by the plan catalog).

## Change plan

1. On the target plan card, click the plan action button (for example upgrade/change — label depends on your current subscription)
2. Confirm in the dialog **You're about to change your subscription**, which shows:
   - Current plan and monthly price
   - New plan and monthly price
   - Note: *The new price will apply starting with your next billing cycle.*
3. Choose **Keep current plan** or confirm the change (upgrade/downgrade label as shown)

{{% alert color="info" title="Modules and access" %}}
Your active plan controls which Analysis modules and capabilities are available. After changing plans, revisit Analysis tools (Resource Catalog, Lock-In, IaC) if access was previously gated.
{{% /alert %}}

## Custom plan

If catalog plans do not fit your needs:

1. Scroll to **Need a Custom Plan?**
2. Click **Request a custom plan**
3. Follow the contact/request flow opened by the app
