**Teams** helps you organize people across your company for ownership, access, and visibility.

Page title: **Teams**  
Subtitle: *Manage and organize teams across your company.*

## Prerequisites

- Access to **Company → Teams**
- Users already invited to the organization (to add as team members)

## How to access

1. Sign in to Cloud2Gether
2. In the left sidebar, expand **Company** → **Teams**
3. Or open: <a href="https://app.cloud2gether.com/teams" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">https://app.cloud2gether.com/teams</a>

## What you see

- **Create new team** button in the page actions
- Search to filter teams
- Teams table with name, tags, created at, and **Options**

Empty state: *No teams yet* — create teams to organize ownership, control access, and gain better visibility across your cloud resources.

## Create a team

1. Click **Create new team**
2. Enter the team **name**
3. Optionally add **Tags** (type a tag and press Enter)
4. Click **Create**

<!-- TODO: Add screenshot — /images/company/create-team.png -->

## Team Options menu

From **Options** on a team row you can:

| Action | What it does |
|--------|----------------|
| **Edit team** | Update name and tags |
| **Manage team members** | Add or remove members |
| **Delete team** | Delete the team after confirmation |

## Manage team members

1. Choose **Manage team members**
2. In the dialog **Manage \<team\> team**, search for a user and click **Add**
3. Review the **Members** list
4. Click **Remove** on a member to remove them (with confirmation)
