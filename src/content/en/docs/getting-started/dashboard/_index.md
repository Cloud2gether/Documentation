The **Dashboard** is your home screen in Cloud2Gether. It summarizes your organization, plan, infrastructure insights, and recommended first steps so you can move quickly from signup to value.

## Prerequisites

- An active Cloud2Gether account — <a href="https://auth.cloud2gether.com/register/profile" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">sign up here</a> if you don't have one
- Permission to view the organization profile (users without access see a permission message on the profile card)

## How to access

1. Sign in to Cloud2Gether
2. In the left sidebar, click **Dashboard**
3. Or open: <a href="https://auth.cloud2gether.com/auth/login/password" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">https://auth.cloud2gether.com/auth/login/password</a>

## What you see

### Organization profile

At the top of the page, the profile card shows:

- Organization name and logo (or initials)
- Current **Plan** name
- An **Upgrade Plan** link (when your plan allows upgrades)
- Quick access to the C2G AI assistant

### First steps on C2G

Three onboarding cards help you get started:

| Card | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Marketplace** | Opens the Cloud2Gether catalog of pre-built solutions (external marketplace) |
| **Cloud Accounts** | Takes you to link and manage cloud provider accounts |
| **Hire an expert** | Coming soon — connect with experts for custom help |

### Infrastructure

The **Infrastructure** section gives an overview of connected providers and detected resources, including:

- **Cloud resources** summary (with options to connect an account or see details)
- **Lock-In Analyzer** preview widget
- **Cloud Stack Analyzer** preview widget

When data is available, a **Last updated** timestamp appears next to the section title.

### Plan and company users

Below infrastructure:

- **Plan** — shows your active plan title, payment amount (or Free), and next payment when available. Use **See plan details** to open the Plans page.
- **Company users** — counts of **Active** and **Pending** users, with a link to the full Users list.

## After a successful checkout

If you complete a plan purchase, Stripe may redirect you back to the Dashboard with a success confirmation. Confirm the dialog and continue exploring the platform.

## Recommended next steps

1. [Link a cloud account](../cloud-accounts/) so Analysis modules can discover your infrastructure
2. Browse the [Marketplace](../marketplace/) for deployable solutions
3. Open **Company → Plans** in the app when you are ready to upgrade (docs for Plans coming soon)
