The **Marketplace** (also labeled **Marketplace** under Solutions in the sidebar) is the Cloud2Gether catalog of pre-built solutions. Use it to discover, evaluate, and start deployments that accelerate your cloud journey.

In the Organization App, Marketplace opens the **external catalog** experience. Deployed or owned solutions are managed separately under **Your Solutions**.

## Prerequisites

- An active Cloud2Gether account

## How to access

1. Sign in to Cloud2Gether
2. In the left sidebar, expand **Solutions** and click **Marketplace**
3. From the Dashboard, use the **Marketplace** first-step card (opens the catalog)

## Marketplace vs Your Solutions

| Area | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Marketplace** | Browse and start solutions from the Cloud2Gether catalog |
| **Your Solutions** | Manage solutions already linked to your organization (details, documentation, billing, execution logs) |

## What to do next

1. Browse solutions that match your use case
2. Follow the catalog flow to start a deployment or purchase
3. Return to the Organization App and open **Solutions → Your Solutions** to track what you own
