The **Analysis** view is the Lock-In hub: KPI-style stats, the list of monitored accounts, and drill-down into a single account’s lock-in categories.

## How to access

1. Open **Analysis → Lock In Analyzer**
2. Open the **Analysis** tab (or go directly after activating monitoring)

URL: `/lock-in-analyzer/analysis`

<!-- TODO: Add screenshot — /images/lockin-analyzer/analysis.png -->

## What you see

- Header with title **Cloud Lock-In Analyzer** and CTA to enable monitoring
- Summary statistics for your monitored estate
- Filters such as search, provider, risk, and status (as shown in the UI)
- Account cards — open one to see category-level lock-in detail

When an account is selected, the page shows that account’s detail. Navigate back to return to the account list.

## Common actions

1. **Enable monitoring** / **Enable New Monitoring** for additional accounts
2. Search and filter monitored accounts
3. Open an account to review lock-in categories and findings
4. Switch to [Compare](../compare/) to contrast insights
