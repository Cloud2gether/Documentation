The **Compare** view helps you understand how your resource inventory changed between sync executions. Use it for drift awareness, audit, and change tracking across your multi-cloud estate.

## How to access

1. Open **Analysis → Resources catalog**
2. Select the **Compare** tab

URL: `/resources-catalog/compare`

<!-- TODO: Add screenshot — /images/resources-catalog/compare.png -->

## Typical workflow

1. Ensure you have at least two meaningful sync executions
2. Open **Compare** and select the executions you want to contrast (as offered in the UI)
3. Review the comparison summary and drill into differences
4. Follow up in **Catalog → Resources** or **Graph** for context
