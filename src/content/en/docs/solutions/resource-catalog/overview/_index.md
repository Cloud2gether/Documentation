The **Catalog** tab is the main inventory experience inside Resource Catalog. It has two inner views: **Overview** and **Resources**.

## How to access

1. Open **Analysis → Resources catalog**
2. Select the **Catalog** tab
3. Choose **Overview** or **Resources** in the inner navigation

URLs:

- Overview: `/resources-catalog/catalog/overview`
- Resources: `/resources-catalog/catalog/resources`

## Overview

Use Overview for a high-level dashboard of your discovered estate — counts and summaries across providers, accounts, services, and regions (as shown in the UI).

Use it to:

- Confirm that syncs have populated data
- Spot concentration of resources by provider or account
- Jump into deeper exploration when something looks unexpected

## Resources

The **Resources** view lists individual cloud resources from your connected accounts. Typical actions include searching, filtering, and opening a resource detail drawer for more information.
